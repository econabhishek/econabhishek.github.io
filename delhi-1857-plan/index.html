About this map

Historical context and method

Published in The Illustrated London News on 24 October 1857, this military plan shows Shahjahanabad and the positions of British forces up to 31 July 1857. It was georeferenced from surviving gates and landmarks; the alignment is approximate because the plan is not a survey-accurate modern map.
