    About the image

    A view of Shahjahanabad at a turning point

    The City of Delhi Before the Siege appeared as a full-page wood engraving in The Illustrated London News on 16 January 1858, about four months after British forces retook Delhi during the uprising of 1857. The image was georeferenced using landmarks identified in its accompanying key. Since the illustration was not drawn to scale, the alignment should be treated as an approximation.

    Read more in the 1858 issue record, Sarmaya’s catalogue entry, and the National Army Museum’s history of the 1857 uprising.
